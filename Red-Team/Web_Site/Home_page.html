What is Blue & Red Team: Cyber Escape Simulator?

Blue & Red Team: Cyber Escape Simulator is an immersive cybersecurity platform where you master both offensive and defensive tactics. Challenge yourself as a Red Team Operator performing web exploitation attacks, or defend critical systems as a Blue Team SOC Analyst. Each scenario is meticulously designed to test your skills, sharpen your instincts, and transform you into a cyber warrior ready for any battlefield!
